# Roadmap

What is being worked on, in order. The dev log (`CHANGELOG.md`) says what has shipped.

## Now

- **First public version.** The README, the website, and a release step that keeps both
  current with the code.
- **First fifteen minutes.** A new owner gets from install to a working model and a first
  app without reading a guide: clearer first run, apps that ship with JimmyHQ marked as
  such, an icon for each app.
- **Real services.** Each connection in the last section below tried against the real
  service, one at a time, and the guide corrected to what was found.

## Next

- **Sharing levels for apps.** Today an app is either open to your agents or fully private.
  A middle level: private, but it may offer one item at a time for you to share, so agents
  can help with a project tracker or parcels without seeing a journal.
- **Cards that do the job.** An app's card on the Bench carries the decision itself (post
  this bill, send this thank-you), not only a link into the app.
- **Enter it once.** A purchase typed in one app can be handed to another (a parcel to
  track, a line in the books) with one click.
- **Search and capture.** One bar that finds tasks, cards, memory and app screens, and
  takes a quick note or task in plain words.
- **Install a set.** Several apps that ship with JimmyHQ installed together, with one
  confirmation.
- **Chat with agents.** The same Chat screen for your models and your agents.
- **Voice.** Spoken commands and spoken replies.

## Later

- **On your phone.** An installable home-screen app with notifications for Bench cards and
  a hold-to-talk capture button.
- **A day thread.** One timeline of today across every app, with decisions inline.
- **A weekly review** the system writes: what came in, what it did alone, what is stuck.
- **Agents that own a project** end to end, with a budget of approvals they may spend.
- **An app store**, with a review of full apps before they are listed.
- **A no-code app builder**: describe an app in chat and get one that keeps and shows
  records.
- **A development workspace** for building apps from inside JimmyHQ.

## Not yet exercised against real services

These connections are written from each service's own documentation and have not yet been
tried against the real service. Each one is off, or in Practice, until you choose it.

- Posts: booking through SocialBu and Buffer ([guide](docs/posts.md))
- Articles: publishing a draft to a WordPress site ([guide](docs/articles.md))
- Packages: looking parcels up through 17TRACK ([guide](docs/packages.md))
- Imagery: pictures from Gemini and from an image server of your own ([guide](docs/imagery.md))
- An agent's own browser on Windows: written, not yet run on a Windows machine
  ([guide](docs/browser.md))
