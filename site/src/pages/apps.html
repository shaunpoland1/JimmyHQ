Apps

Apps, and what each one asks of you.

An app adds a screen, a job or a tool to JimmyHQ. Before one installs you read what it may do, in the sentences below, and say yes. This page is built from each app's own manifest, so it always matches the version in the code.

The three kinds of app

described

A manifest and nothing else: tables and views. It keeps and shows records and has no code.

sandboxed

A page in a locked frame, with no network of its own. It can do only what its permissions list, through one bridge.

full

A program that runs as you. It can do anything you can, and JimmyHQ cannot limit it. Install only the ones you trust.

An app marked private asks only a model on your own machines, and keeps what it holds off your record.
