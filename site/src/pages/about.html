About

A headquarters you own.

More and more of the work people do goes through models and agents. JimmyHQ is a place to run that work yourself: on your own computer, with the models you choose, where every agent and app answers to one person and every decision goes on one record you can read.

It is built in public, under the Apache-2.0 licence. The code, the guides and the plan are all in the repository, and the backlog says what is done and what is not.

It is early. Most areas work, and the guides are honest about what does not yet. Some connections to outside services have only been written from their documentation. If you try it, expect rough edges, and please say what you find.

Where it came from

Version 3 is new, and this is the first time the code is public. Most of what is in it is not new. JimmyHQ has been more than eight months in the making, and it has been rebuilt as it grew.

It began as experiments with OpenClaw. That grew into JimmyHQ version 1, and then version 2, each one built around a single person's own machines and habits. The Bench, the Tape, the Village and memory took their shape over those months of real use, and so did the rule that nothing goes out without a click.

Version 3 keeps what proved its worth and is written again from the start, so that other people can run the same things on their own systems. Nothing personal is in the code, anything that could differ between two people is a setting, and the apps are kept apart from the platform.
